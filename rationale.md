# C-Pay Brand Mark Rationale

## The Problem
The original C-Pay logo featured a Bitcoin symbol (₿) at its center. This presented two critical issues:
1. **Factual Inaccuracy:** C-Pay operates on the Stellar network, not Bitcoin.
2. **Positioning Failure:** The core product thesis is "no crypto knowledge required" — a seamless, invisible blockchain experience akin to standard fiat systems like UPI. Using the most recognizable cryptocurrency symbol directly contradicts this thesis. It introduces connotations of speculation, volatility, and high taxation, which are fatal for a remittance product targeting everyday users.

## The Concept: "The Arc of Transfer"
The new brand mark is built around a single, unified concept: **The C is the transfer.**

- **The Arc:** The letterform 'C' is rendered as a geometric arc, an incomplete circle. This represents an opening, a pathway where money flows freely, breaking down the traditional closed loops of legacy financial systems.
- **The Gap & Terminal:** Money moves through the gap. The arc concludes with a clear directional terminal (an arrow) pointing forward. This creates a strong sense of movement across borders and boundaries, perfectly encapsulating the essence of a remittance product.

## Design Constraints Met
- **Monochrome Resilience:** The mark is designed in a single solid color (black). There are no gradients, shading, or complex overlapping shapes. This guarantees flawless reproduction in harsh, low-fidelity environments, such as being printed on a receipt in cheap black ink.
- **Geometric Purity:** Constructed purely from a circular arc and straight lines with a uniform stroke weight. It rejects detailed illustration in favor of bold, structural geometry.
- **Micro-Legibility:** The uniform, heavy stroke weight (120px on a 1024px canvas) ensures the shape remains perfectly distinguishable and readable at 16px favicon sizes. The arrow terminal remains crisp and recognizable even when scaled down significantly.
- **Crypto-Agnostic:** The mark relies strictly on typographic geometry and universal symbols of movement (the arrow). It entirely avoids chains, nodes, or specific currency symbols, aligning perfectly with the "fiat-first" user experience.
